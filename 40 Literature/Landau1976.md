## Reading notes

A compact, Lagrangian treatment of small oscillations. The sections on free oscillations, forced oscillations, damped oscillations and forced oscillations under friction derive the same results as this series in a few pages each, including the resonance curve of the absorbed energy and its half-width near resonance, and go on to anharmonic and parametric resonance.

Landau and Lifshitz write the damping term as $2\lambda\dot x$, so their $\lambda$ is our $\gamma$, and they use $\gamma$ for the drive frequency, which we call $\omega$.
