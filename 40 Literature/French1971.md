## Reading notes

An introductory but careful treatment of oscillations that builds from the free harmonic oscillator through damping and forced vibrations to coupled oscillators and waves. Its chapters on free damped motion and on forced vibrations and resonance cover the same ground as this series, with many experimental examples and a strong emphasis on energy and the quality factor.

Notation differs from this collection: French writes the damping term as $\gamma\dot x$ with $\gamma=b/m$, so his $Q=\omega_0/\gamma$ equals our $Q=\omega_0/2\gamma$. Translate the damping symbol, not the results.
