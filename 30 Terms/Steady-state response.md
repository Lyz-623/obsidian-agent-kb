> [!definition] Steady-state response
> The motion $|A|\cos(\omega t-\delta)$ at the drive frequency that a driven damped oscillator settles into once the transient has decayed; its amplitude and lag depend only on $\omega$, $\omega_0$ and $\gamma$.
