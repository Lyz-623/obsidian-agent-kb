> [!definition] Impulse response
> The motion $G(t)=\mathrm{e}^{-\gamma t}\sin(\omega_d t)/\omega_d$ ($t\ge0$) of an oscillator at rest after a unit kick; by superposition it gives the response to any force, $x=\frac{1}{m}\int G(t-t')F(t')\,\mathrm{d}t'$.

Explained in [[Impulse response and arbitrary forces#An impulsive force|Impulse response and arbitrary forces · An impulsive force]].
