Every article follows the conventions below. When a cited source uses a different convention, the article states the translation; the literature notes record each source's own symbols.

## Symbols and conventions

### The oscillator

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $x(t)$ | displacement from equilibrium |
| $m$, $k$, $b$ | mass, spring stiffness, linear drag coefficient (drag force $-b\dot x$) |
| $\omega_0=\sqrt{k/m}$ | natural angular frequency; free period $T_0=2\pi/\omega_0$ |
| $\gamma=b/2m$ | damping rate: amplitude decays as $\mathrm{e}^{-\gamma t}$, energy as $\mathrm{e}^{-2\gamma t}$ |
| $\omega_d=\sqrt{\omega_0^2-\gamma^2}$ | damped angular frequency of free oscillation |
| $Q=\omega_0/2\gamma$ | quality factor; $Q=1/2$ is critical damping |

### The drive

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $F_0\cos\omega t$ | driving force with amplitude $F_0$ and angular frequency $\omega$ |
| $x=\operatorname{Re}\,[A\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}\omega t}]$ | complex notation; $A$ is the complex amplitude |
| $\delta$ | phase lag of the displacement behind the force, $0\le\delta\le\pi$ |
| $\bar P$ | power delivered by the force, averaged over one cycle |
| $\Delta\omega_{\mathrm{FWHM}}$ | full width at half maximum of $\bar P(\omega)$ |

## Writing conventions

### Numbering and references

Key results are numbered per article as Eq. (1), Eq. (2), …; figures as Fig. 1, Fig. 2, …. A reference to another article names it, as in "Free damped oscillations" Eq. (4). Frequencies are angular frequencies (rad/s) unless stated otherwise, and figures plot time in units of $T_0$ and frequency in units of $\omega_0$.
