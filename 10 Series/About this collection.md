These notes are a small, complete example of an explainer knowledge base: one question per article, every key result derived, figures computed from scripts, and every concept and source given its own note. The topic, the damped and driven harmonic oscillator, is deliberately classic so that every claim can be checked against standard textbooks ([[French1971|French 1971]]; [[Landau1976|Landau and Lifshitz 1976]]).

## How the notes are organised

### Entry points

The entry point is [[Home]]. The core of the collection is 2 articles in 1 series, listed in reading order. Each series answers one question, and its series map explains how the articles answer it together:

- [[1 Damped and driven oscillations|"Damped and driven oscillations"]]: how a damped oscillator moves on its own and how it answers a periodic force.

### Terms and sources

Bold links in the articles point to term cards. Each card gives a one-sentence definition of a single concept and links to the article that explains it; together the cards form the concept graph of the collection. Citations point to literature notes, which hold bibliographic data and reading notes. Symbols and sign conventions shared by all articles are listed in [[Notation and conventions]].

## How the notes are maintained

### Agents and checks

Articles are written and revised by Claude Code agents using the `kb-notes` skill, following the vault conventions in `90 System/CONVENTIONS.md`. After every edit the series maps are regenerated with `90 System/outline-sync.py`, and cross-references, links and frontmatter counts are checked by the skill's scripts.
